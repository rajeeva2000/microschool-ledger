Family portal

Give families clarity without creating more admin work.

The Microschool Ledger Family Portal gives parents a calm, self-service view of tuition, payments, invoices, and enrollments. It removes a whole category of repetitive admin emails and helps families feel informed instead of uncertain.

Parents can check their balance, review what each student is enrolled in, pull up an invoice on their own, and RSVP to upcoming field trips — without creating data-entry risk or forcing your staff into the middle of every question.

Families can also pay online directly through the portal — credit card, ACH bank transfer, Apple Pay, or Google Pay via Stripe. Autopay enrollment is available so monthly tuition charges settle automatically, with a dunning sequence that handles failed payments before they become a collections issue.

Two-household families are covered: a family can authorize more than one parent email, so each parent gets their own sign-in to the same account. And for families who would rather not log in, you can send a secure payment link — it opens a no-login payment page that always shows the family's current balance, so a parent can pay in a couple of taps straight from a reminder or invoice email.

Getting families in is effortless: a parent signs in with any email address you already have on file— there's no separate invitation to send and track first, so you can switch the portal on for every family at once. Once in, they can keep their own contact details current from a self-service My Info tab — parent names, email, phone, mailing address, a second guardian, and how they'd like to hear from you — and their corrections flow straight back into your records. Need the whole roster refreshed before a new year? Send the built-in "Confirm your family info" campaign: every family gets a private, no-login link to the same form, and you can test it on one family before the full send.

"The portal eliminates the category of emails that ask, 'What do I owe?' or 'Can you resend our invoice?' Parents can check for themselves — and pay." Self-service by design. Clear for families. Less work for your staff.

Built for private schools using Step-Up

The accounting records your Step-Up AUP requires — ready before the auditor asks.

Florida statute requires every private school receiving Step-Up scholarship funds to demonstrate an adequate accounting system, proper classification of scholarship deposits, and documented educational expenditures. Schools receiving more than $250,000 must contract a licensed CPA for a formal AUP engagement under Florida Statute 1002.421(1)(q). Microschool Ledger's P&L, expense tracking, Step-Up reconciliation, and audit log satisfy all three criteria — and the Export for CPA button generates the exact workbook your auditor needs.

  • AUP Compliance tab: auto-checks whether school info, expenses, and scholarship records are complete
  • Export for CPA: one-click Excel workbook (AUP Summary, Scholarship Funds, Expense Detail by Category)
  • Amber threshold warning when your Step-Up total approaches the $250,000 CPA-required level
$250K The Step-Up threshold that triggers a mandatory CPA audit under Florida Statute 1002.421(1)(q). MSLedger gives your auditor a clean, complete record to work from.

From first inquiry to enrolled family — in one system

A full admissions pipeline that hands off directly to billing.

Most schools run admissions in a spreadsheet and billing in another tool — which means every new family requires double data entry and a manual handoff. Microschool Ledger's admissions pipeline connects inquiry to enrollment with no re-keying: a public interest form captures leads, a six-step application workflow moves applicants through the funnel, and a single "Convert to Family" action creates the family record, assigns sibling order, and opens the enrollment wizard.

  • Public interest form with configurable questions — families self-serve into your pipeline
  • Two-stage funnel: Leads (interest submitted) → Applicants (application in progress) → Enrolled
  • Convert to Family: one click turns an accepted applicant into a billable family record
  • A calm working queue — status changes are explicit and instantly undoable, "Needs attention" sorting surfaces what to do next, no accidental stage changes
  • The full application form is configurable too — hide any section or individual question that doesn't apply to your school, no code changes
