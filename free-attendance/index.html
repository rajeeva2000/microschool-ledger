Questions

Good questions to ask first

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in the web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install.

Can I look back at past days and fix mistakes?

Yes. History shows every saved day. You can open any day, correct it and save again, and the day shows who saved it last. You can download your records as a spreadsheet file (CSV) at any time.

What happens at 25 students?

The free plan holds 25 active students. Students who have left can be archived to make room, and their past attendance stays in your history. If your school needs more places, email info@microschoolledger.com.

Do I need a credit card?

No. There is no card to enter and no demo call to book. Sign up with Google, or with any email address and a password.

What isn't included?

The free account is attendance only. Tuition billing, family payments, Florida Step-Up for Students reconciliation, the family portal and field trip RSVPs are part of the complete Microschool Ledger plan.

Who can see my students' information?

Only the people you add to your school's account. We don't sell your school's data, and student names are never sent to our usage analytics. See our Privacy Policy.

Can my teachers take attendance too?

Yes. Add team members from Setup, and they sign in with their own email.

Who do I ask for help?

Email info@microschoolledger.com. A real person will reply.
