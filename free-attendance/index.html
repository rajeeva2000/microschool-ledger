About the 25-student limit

"Active" means students you're currently tracking. Archive students who have left and their places open up; their past attendance stays in your history.

If you reach 25, you keep your account, your records and your history. You just can't add another active student.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is MSL Attendance really free?

Yes. MSL Attendance is free for schools with up to 25 active students. There is no credit card and no required demo.

What happens when I reach 25 students?

You keep using your account, and all of your student records and attendance history stay available. You can't add a 26th active student. Archiving a student who has left frees a place, and their past attendance stays in your history.

Do I have to schedule a demo?

No. Sign-up is self-service. You can create your account and start without talking to a salesperson.

Do I need to buy the full Microschool Ledger platform?

No. MSL Attendance stands on its own. Need billing too? The complete Microschool Ledger plan handles tuition, payments and Florida Step-Up for Students scholarships. See pricing.

Can I use it if my school doesn't meet every day?

Yes. You choose the days your school meets and your time zone.

Can I record late arrivals and early pickups?

Yes. You can note when a student arrived late or left early, and who picked them up.

Can families RSVP to field trips and events?

That's MSL Events, a second free tool that uses the same list of families and students. You can switch it on in your school's settings.

Can I export attendance records?

Yes. Attendance history can be downloaded as a CSV file at any time.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It works in the web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install.

What kind of support is available?

Email support at info@microschoolledger.com. A real person reads every message.

Is student information secure?

Your student list is visible to the people you add to your school's account, and to Microschool Ledger only when needed to run and support the service. We don't sell your school's data, and student names are never sent to our usage analytics. Our Privacy Policy explains how data is handled, kept and deleted.
