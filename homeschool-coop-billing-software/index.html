Why generic tools fall short

Your co-op is not a simple invoice business.

Most billing tools assume one customer, one service, and one payment stream. A homeschool co-op has families, students, programs, discounts, school years, and often several ways money arrives.

⌂

You bill families, not isolated students

Parents need one understandable account even when siblings attend different programs at different prices.

≋

Your programs do not all bill the same way

Annual tuition, semester classes, workshops, registration, materials, and field trips all need to reach the same family balance.

↔

Payments arrive through different channels

Checks, Stripe, ACH, autopay, and scholarship disbursements should reduce the same balance without manual cross-referencing.

◷

Your year overlaps with itself

Next-year deposits can arrive while this year’s tuition is still being collected. Academic-year context has to stay attached.

✉

Parents want answers quickly

A portal lets families check balances, invoices, and payment history without sending another email to your administrator.

◇

Your administrator has another job

The person managing billing may also be teaching, planning programs, and talking with families. The system has to feel calm and clear.

Common questions

What co-op administrators usually ask.

Can one family account include more than one student?

Yes. The family has one account and one understandable balance, while each student keeps their own enrollment, program, and charge detail.

Can it handle sibling discounts?

Yes. Sibling pricing can be configured by program and family type so the correct annual charge is generated without recalculating it by hand.

Can families pay online?

Yes. The family portal supports Stripe payments by card, ACH, Apple Pay, or Google Pay, along with autopay and secure no-login payment links.

Can we keep using checks or other offline payments?

Yes. Your team can record manual payments alongside online and scholarship payments, and they all reduce the same family balance.

Do we have to set it up ourselves?

No. Hands-on onboarding is included. We help configure your programs and billing rules and bring in the family and student data you already maintain.
