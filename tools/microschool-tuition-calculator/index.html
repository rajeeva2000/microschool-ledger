How to use your estimate

Set tuition with the whole year in view.

Start with the cost of running your school

Include your own compensation, staff, space, and recurring expenses. Compare the tuition you expect to receive with what your school needs to spend, not just the price of nearby schools.

Allow for the discounts you actually offer

Estimate the combined annual effect of family, program-combination, and sibling discounts for each program. For example, $6,000 of discounts on $60,000 of program tuition is a 10% allowance. Enter it once.

Check timing as well as totals

Tuition can cover annual costs while leaving a quiet month short of cash. Compare deposits and payment months with your full year of expenses. Scholarship receipts replace family payments toward tuition; they are not extra tuition revenue.

What this calculator assumes
  • One school year of tuition and 12 months of operating costs. Enrollment and costs stay constant; there are no midyear withdrawals or additions.
  • Deposits arrive upfront. Remaining payments begin in month 1. Different program start dates, late receipts, and scholarship disbursement dates are not modeled.
  • The same collection percentage and blended payment-cost rate apply to every receipt, including deposits. Actual methods and fees may vary.
  • Annual operating costs are spread evenly over 12 months. Startup purchases, taxes, debt payments, grants, and cash already on hand are excluded.
  • Discounts use one estimated percentage per program. The calculator does not apply family-level rules or check which students take multiple programs.
  • Dollar amounts round to cents. The final payment can differ slightly so the deposit and payments equal the annual tuition after discounts.
  • This is a planning estimate, not a quote or a guarantee of enrollment, collections, or financial results. Your entries are not saved when you leave or reload this page.
