The Problem

Generic tools weren't built
for how you operate.

Microschools and homeschool co-ops have a financial structure unlike any other organization. Off-the-shelf tools handle none of it well.

You bill families, not students
Tuition is a family relationship with sibling discount tiers that vary by program. Most billing tools don't understand this — they invoice per person and leave you to do the math.
Two payment streams, one balance
Family checks and Florida Step-Up scholarship disbursements arrive separately, on different schedules, and need to be reconciled against the same tuition balance. Manually.
Multiple programs, multiple rates
A family might have one child in the Core Program and another in a Film Workshop — each with its own pricing, schedule, and payment plan. Spreadsheets can't keep up.
Your admin is also your teacher
There's no billing department. The person chasing overdue invoices is the same person teaching a class in an hour. Every minute spent on admin is a minute taken from students.
Collections are always reactive
You find out a family is two months behind when you happen to check the spreadsheet. There's no dashboard, no flag, no way to see who owes what at a glance.
Scholarship compliance is manual
Step-Up invoices need specific compliance language. Families need documentation for reimbursement. Building those statements by hand, every billing cycle, is hours of work that adds no value.

🎓 Florida Step-Up for Students

The killer feature for Florida co-ops.

For Florida microschools, Step-Up scholarship payments represent a significant portion of tuition revenue. Manually entering disbursements from the state portal — matching them to students, reconciling against invoices, attributing to the right program — can take hours every billing cycle. Microschool Ledger eliminates that entirely.

  • 1
    Export payment history from the Step-Up portal as a tab-delimited file
  • 2
    Paste directly into Microschool Ledger — no file conversion or column mapping
  • 3
    Payments are automatically matched to students using their sequence number
  • 4
    Program attribution is automatic — Film Workshop vs. Core Program handled by category
  • 5
    Re-imports are safe — existing records are deduplicated, missing fields backfilled
