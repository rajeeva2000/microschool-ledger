Why generic tools fail

Your school doesn't operate like a dentist's office or a tutoring business.

Most billing tools assume one customer, one invoice, one payment stream. Microschools and co-ops are messier — and much more human.

You bill families, not students

The paying relationship is with the family, but the services are delivered to individual students across different programs with sibling discount rules.

Direct payments and scholarships collide

A family might pay by check while Florida Step-Up sends a separate disbursement days later. Both need to land against the same balance without manual guesswork.

Your school year doesn't match the calendar year

Deposits for next year can arrive while you're still collecting this year’s monthly tuition. General-purpose accounting tools don’t understand that overlap.

Your administrator is also your teacher

You don’t have a billing department. You need a system that feels calm, clear, and usable between classes — not enterprise software that adds more cognitive load.

Florida Step-Up

What used to take hours now takes minutes.

Paste the provider portal export directly into Microschool Ledger. Payments match by student sequence number, scholarship credits land on the right family, and re-imports stay clean.

  1. 1
    Export payment history from the Step-Up portalNo column mapping, no file conversion, no cleanup project first.
  2. 2
    Paste it into Microschool LedgerThe import flow reads the tab-delimited export directly and filters to paid records.
  3. 3
    Match payments by student sequence numberNot by fragile name matching — by the actual field that matters.
  4. 4
    Auto-attribute by program and deduplicate on re-importSo the same disbursement doesn’t get counted twice and missing program data can be backfilled.
