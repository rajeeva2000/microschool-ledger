Why MSL Events

"Who's coming?" shouldn't take twenty messages.

A field trip or a class party usually means a group text, a sign-up sheet and a list of who still owes money, kept in someone's head or notebook.

Families miss the message, answers get buried, and the day before the trip you're still counting heads.

MSL Events keeps every RSVP, reminder and payment note for an event on one page.

How it works

Get started today.

  1. Create your free school account Enter your school's name and sign up with Google or any email address. No sales call, demo or credit card.
  2. Add your families and create an event Add each family and its children, then create an event with its date, cost, capacity and RSVP-by date.
  3. Invite families and send reminders Email invites to the families you choose and remind the ones who haven't answered. Each email carries the family's own RSVP link.
  4. Families RSVP from their phone Families open their link, see the event and its cost, and answer. You see every answer on the event page.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is MSL Events really free?

Yes. MSL Events is free for schools with up to 25 active students. There is no credit card and no required demo.

Does MSL Events collect payment from families?

No. MSL Events never takes payments. Each event shows its cost, families pay your school however you already collect money (cash, check, Venmo and so on), and you tick Paid. If a family adds another child after paying, the rest still shows as owed.

Do families need an account to RSVP?

No. Each family gets its own RSVP link that works without signing in. Families can also answer from your school's family portal.

What happens when an event is full?

You set a capacity for each event. Once it's full, families who say they're coming go on a waitlist, and you can confirm them if a spot opens.

Can it send invites and reminders for me?

Yes. You can email an invite to the families you choose and remind every family that hasn't answered. You can also turn on automatic reminders before your RSVP-by date and a day-before email to families who are coming. Families can unsubscribe from these emails.

What happens when I reach 25 students?

You keep your account, your families, your events and their history. You can't add a 26th active student. Archiving a student who has left frees a place.

Can I use it with MSL Attendance?

Yes. MSL Events and MSL Attendance share one list of families and students. You can switch either one on or off in your school's settings, and switching one off only hides it; nothing is deleted.

Do I need to buy the full Microschool Ledger platform?

No. MSL Events stands on its own. If you later want help with tuition billing and payments, the complete Microschool Ledger plan is described on our pricing page.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It works in the web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, for your staff and for families. There is nothing to install.

What kind of support is available?

Email support at info@microschoolledger.com. A real person reads every message.

Is family information secure?

Your families and students are visible to the people you add to your school's account, and to Microschool Ledger only when needed to run and support the service. Each family sees only its own events and children. We don't sell your school's data, and names are never sent to our usage analytics. Our Privacy Policy explains how data is handled, kept and deleted.
