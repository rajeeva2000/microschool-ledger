Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is MSL Events really free?

Yes. MSL Events is free for schools with up to 25 active students. There is no credit card and no required demo.

Do families need an account?

No. Each family gets its own personal RSVP link by email. They open it, choose which children are coming and how many adults, and answer. Families who already use your school's parent portal can answer there too.

Does MSL Events collect payments?

No. You show the cost per child and per adult, families pay you the way they already do (cash, check, Venmo or similar), and you tick Paid for each family. MSL Events doesn't take payments or charge a fee.

Can I limit how many people come?

Yes. Set a capacity and an adults-per-family limit. When the event is full, new answers go on a waitlist, and you can move a family off the waitlist when a place opens.

What emails does it send?

Invites with each family's personal link, a reminder to families who haven't answered, a confirmation when a family answers, and, if you leave automatic reminders on, a reminder before your RSVP date and a day-before email to families who are coming.

Can I use MSL Events and MSL Attendance together?

Yes. Both are free for up to 25 active students and share one list of families and students. Switch either one on or off in Setup.

Can I download the attendee list?

Yes. Each event's list of who's coming, with headcounts and who has paid, downloads as a CSV file.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, for you and for families. It works in the web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install.

What kind of support is available?

Email support at info@microschoolledger.com. A real person reads every message.
