Common questions

A few things you may be wondering.

Does this replace QuickBooks?

No. Microschool Ledger manages the family-level operational detail that is awkward to maintain in accounting software and can work alongside QuickBooks.

Is this only for Step Up schools?

No. It manages tuition, family billing, payments, statements, and reporting for microschools broadly. Its Step Up workflow is especially deep because it was built in Florida.

Will we have to change our entire process?

No. The conversation starts with what you do today. The goal is to simplify the disconnected parts, not force a wholesale change on day one.

Is this a sales presentation?

It is a practical 20-minute conversation. We'll ask about your current process and show only the parts of Microschool Ledger that are relevant.
