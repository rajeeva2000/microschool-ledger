Why generic billing tools break down for small schools
Most accounting tools think in terms of one client, one invoice, one payment stream. Microschools and homeschool co-ops work differently: families span multiple students, sibling discounts vary by program, scholarship disbursements arrive separately, and the person doing billing is often also teaching.

You bill families, not students

One family may have multiple students, multiple programs, and different pricing rules tied to sibling order.

Scholarships complicate the ledger

Florida Step-Up credits arrive separately and still need to land against the same family balance as checks or Stripe payments.

Parents want answers fast

”What do I owe?” and “Did my payment go through?” become constant admin interruptions unless families can check for themselves.

Enrichment providers charge differently

Over 54% of Florida Step-Up providers are enrichment or elective programs that charge flat semester fees — not annual tuition grids. Generic tools aren't built for that model.

A closer look at the core workflow

Enrollment & tuition

Guided enrollment flow, automatic monthly charge generation, deposits, payment plans, sibling discounts, and support for overlapping school years.

Flat charges & semester courses

Flat fees for workshops and elective classes. Short-term programs with term-based enrollment (Fall / Spring) and a per-term roster view — no annual grid needed.

Payments & collections

Manual payment entry, Stripe imports, Step-Up credits, customizable invoices, and automated reminders with a preview-before-send queue.

Family portal

A self-service portal where parents see balances, pay online via Stripe (card, ACH, Apple Pay, Google Pay), view payment history, access invoices, and sign required documents (handbook, permission forms) — without emailing your team. Two-household families can add a second parent, and no-login payment links let families pay straight from a reminder.

Reporting & financial health

Revenue, expenses, budgets, and cash flow view — know exactly which months you're behind on collections and by how much.
