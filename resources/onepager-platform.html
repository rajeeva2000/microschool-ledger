Instead of stitching together spreadsheets, payment history, and family notes, Microschool Ledger gives administrators one home base for what’s been collected, what’s due, and what needs attention next.

What you get

  • Family-based billing built for the real structure of a microschool
  • Automatic tuition charges from enrollments and payment plans
  • Flat charges and semester courses for enrichment and elective programs
  • Florida Step-Up import and scholarship credit tracking
  • Collections with automated reminders and preview-before-send
  • Custom invoice title, payment instructions, and reply-to per school
  • Passwordless family portal for balances, invoices, and payment history

Why it’s different

This wasn’t designed in theory. It was built to solve real operator problems: sibling discounts, overlapping school years, scholarship payments arriving separately, and parents needing clarity without creating more admin work.
