Frequently asked questions

Simple answers for a focused financial system.

Microschool Ledger is designed to handle the financial side of a microschool clearly and simply — tuition, payments, scholarships, family balances, and parent visibility.

1. What problem does Microschool Ledger solve?

It replaces spreadsheets and manual tracking for tuition, payments, scholarships, and family balances so microschools can see where things stand clearly.

2. How is this different from other school management tools?

Most school management systems try to do everything — curriculum tracking, attendance, communication, and billing all at once. Microschool Ledger focuses only on financial clarity:

  • tuition
  • payments
  • scholarships
  • balances

That makes it simpler, easier to use, and better aligned to what many microschools actually need.

3. How do Florida Step-Up payments work?

Florida Step-Up for Students payments can be imported directly from the provider portal export, matched to students by sequence number, and tracked alongside direct family payments — so you see one clear balance instead of managing separate records.

4. Does it handle academic years vs. calendar years?

Microschool Ledger is built around academic years and supports overlap periods — such as deposits for the next school year arriving while the current one is still active.

5. Can I use custom pricing or discounts?

Yes. Tuition can be overridden per student as needed, which makes it possible to handle custom pricing, sibling discounts, or special cases without workarounds.

6. Is there a parent portal?

Yes. Parents can see their balance, review what each student is enrolled in, view payment history, and access invoices — all on their own, without emailing your admin.

7. Do parents need passwords?

No. The family portal uses passwordless sign-in via secure email links. Parents click a link, they're in.

8. How is data secured?

Data is stored in Google Cloud with strict access controls. The system is designed to store only what's needed to run the financial workflow clearly and safely.

9. Who owns the data?

Your school owns its data. You can export it at any time.

10. Can I still use QuickBooks?

Yes. Data can be exported if you want Microschool Ledger to handle day-to-day financial clarity while QuickBooks handles formal accounting.

11. How does onboarding work?

We do hands-on setup with each school — initial configuration, importing key data, and a guided walkthrough so the system is ready to use from day one.
