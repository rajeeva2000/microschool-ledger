What parents can do

  • See a clear balance for the current school year
  • Review total tuition, payments, and amount still due
  • Open their invoice without waiting on staff
  • Check each student’s enrollments and annual rate
  • Review payment history in one calm, simple place

Why schools love it

The portal reduces repetitive parent questions, improves trust around billing, and gives your school a cleaner boundary between account visibility and admin effort. It’s read-only by design, so parents get clarity without creating data integrity risk.
