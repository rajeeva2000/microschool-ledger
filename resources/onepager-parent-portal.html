Invoices & payments

From “What do I owe?” to paid—in the same place.

The portal keeps the explanation beside the action. Families can review their account first, then find the right invoice or make a payment without waiting for your staff to resend anything.

See the full account story

Parents can look beyond a single balance and understand how the family arrived there.

  • Current balance and total paid
  • Charges organized by student and program
  • Direct payments and Florida Step-Up for Students credits
  • Payment history across school years

Open and print invoices

Families can pull up invoices when they need them for their own records or scholarship documentation.

  • Self-service invoice access
  • Clear program and student detail
  • Printable documentation without an admin email
  • School-specific payment instructions

Pay online

Families can move from reviewing their balance to paying it without leaving the account.

  • Credit and debit cards through Stripe
  • ACH bank transfer
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay on supported devices
  • Autopay for recurring tuition charges

Pay without signing in

When speed matters, your school can send a secure payment link that opens the family’s current balance directly.

  • No-login payment page
  • Current balance shown at the time the link opens
  • Useful in reminders and invoice emails
  • A few taps from message to payment
Field trips & events

Families can decide, RSVP, and pay without a separate form.

Upcoming experiences live beside the family account, so trip details and financial details stay connected.

Review the trip

Parents see the date, description, cost, capacity, and the students who are eligible.

Choose who is attending

The family RSVPs for the appropriate student without your staff copying names from another tool.

Pay the trip charge

When payment is required, the family can handle it as part of the same clear flow.

Join the waitlist

If a trip reaches capacity, overflow moves to an automatic waitlist instead of an admin-managed queue.

Real family structures

One family record can support more than one parent.

Two-household and shared-care families should not have to funnel every account question through one person. Your school can authorize more than one parent email, giving each parent a separate sign-in to the same family account.

Separate access. One source of truth.

Each authorized parent sees the same balances, invoices, payments, and family activity—without duplicating the family record.

Families can keep their own information current

The My Info area lets parents review and correct the household details your school relies on.

  • Parent and guardian names
  • Primary and additional email addresses
  • Phone numbers and mailing address
  • Second guardian information
  • Communication preferences

Before a new school year, your school can also send a private “Confirm your family info” link to every family and test the campaign with one household first.

Required documents

Sign the handbook, permission forms, and policies without a single sheet of paper.

Whatever your school requires each year — the handbook acknowledgment, a photo/video permission, a technology-use policy, or a document you configure yourself — families complete it right from the same account.

See what's needed

A family's Documents tab lists exactly what's outstanding for them this year — no guessing which paper form applies.

Read, then sign

Families read the exact document text — with the source PDF attached when your school provides one — and sign electronically.

Get a permanent record

Every signature is rendered and hashed into a tamper-evident record, so you always know exactly what a family agreed to and when.

Keep a copy

The family gets an emailed confirmation and can download their signed copy anytime — and so can your staff, from the compliance view.

What your school gets back

Self-service for families means fewer interruptions for staff.

The portal is not a separate communication project. It is the family-facing side of the same billing, enrollment, trip, and household information your school already manages in Microschool Ledger.

  • Fewer repetitive account emails Families can answer common balance, invoice, and payment questions themselves.
  • Cleaner records Household corrections flow back into the information your staff uses.
  • Field trips stay connected RSVPs, capacity, charges, and payments remain part of one system.
  • A more confident family experience Parents can see what happened, what is next, and what they can do about it.
Common questions

Family Portal FAQ

Do families need to create a password?

No. Families use a passwordless sign-in with an email address already connected to their family record.

Can more than one parent access the same account?

Yes. A family can authorize more than one parent email so each parent can use their own sign-in for the same family account.

Can families pay through the portal?

Yes. Families can pay by card, ACH bank transfer, Apple Pay, or Google Pay through Stripe. Schools can also offer autopay and secure no-login payment links.

Can families manage field trips there too?

Yes. Families can review upcoming field trips, RSVP, pay when a trip has a charge, and join an automatic waitlist when capacity is full.
